# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Homeowners and office/commercial buyers in Kenya looking for ready-made curtains for their spaces.

## Product Purpose
Provide high-clarity viewing of ready-made curtains with exact fixed dimensions, real-world scale reference, and light transmission preview, enabling customers to verify suitability and contact the owner directly via WhatsApp or phone call.

## Positioning
Ready-made, fixed-size quality curtains with nationwide Kenyan delivery, direct personal phone confirmation of stock, and immediate scale & light preview before buying.

## Operating Context
Browsing primarily on mobile devices (including mid-range Android devices on metered Safaricom/Airtel connections) and desktop. Customer checks curtain drop and width against their room, then triggers a pre-filled WhatsApp message or direct phone call.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Fixed-size ready-made curtains with explicit dimensions (width and drop in cm).
- Fixed prices in KES.
- No custom sizing, stitching, or measurement service.
- No cart, online payment, or automated checkout; availability is confirmed by phone/WhatsApp.
- Nationwide delivery across Kenya (courier/method TODO confirm with owner).
- Ready-made stock packaging (pairs vs single panels TODO confirm with owner).
- Recommended coverage range (TODO confirm with owner).
- Fabric care instructions (TODO confirm with owner).

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Tari
- Clear, honest representation of scale and fabric light transmission.
- Direct, warm Kenyan commerce communication via phone and WhatsApp.

## Product Principles
1. Truth in Dimensions: What you see is accurately scaled relative to real architectural and human reference points.
2. Light Transparency: The visualizer separates actual physical fabric light transmission from UI rendering.
3. Frictionless Inquiry: Every detail (SKU, color, dimensions, KES price) is pre-formatted for instant WhatsApp/call order placement.
